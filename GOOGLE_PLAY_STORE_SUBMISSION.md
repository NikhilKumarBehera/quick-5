1. **Go to Google Play Console**

2. **Create New App**

   - Fill in details:
     - **App name:** Quick 5
     - **Default language:** English (United States)
     - **App or game:** App
     - **Free or paid:** Free
   - Accept declarations:
     - ☑️ Developer Program Policies
     - ☑️ US export laws
   - Click "Create app"

## ❓ Common Issues & Solutions

### Issue: "App bundle contains unused code"
**Solution:** This is just a warning. You can ignore it or enable R8 full mode (already done in your build.gradle).

### Issue: "Missing privacy policy"
**Solution:** Host PRIVACY_POLICY.md online and add URL to store listing.

### Issue: "Screenshots required"
**Solution:** Upload at least 2 screenshots (1080x1920). Follow guide above.

### Issue: "Feature graphic required"
**Solution:** Create 1024x500 graphic with app name and visuals.

### Issue: "Content rating pending"
**Solution:** Complete content rating questionnaire in Play Console.

### Issue: "App signing error"
**Solution:** Your app is already signed correctly. If you see this, re-run build-release.sh.
