1. Introduction

Welcome to Quick 5! This Privacy Policy explains how Quick 5 ("we," "our," or "the app") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

🔒 Privacy-First Design: Quick 5 is built with privacy at its core. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. All your data stays on your device.

2. Data We DO NOT Collect

Quick 5 does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal Information: No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any identifying information
  • Location Data: We don't track or access your location
  • Device Identifiers: No device IDs, advertising IDs, or unique identifiers
  • IP Addresses: We don't collect or log IP addresses
  • Usage Analytics: No analytics, tracking, or behavior monitoring
  • Advertising Data: We don't use advertising networks or track ad interactions
  • Cookies: No cookies or similar tracking technologies
  • Third-Party Data: We don't integrate with any third-party data collection services

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Quick 5 stores the following data locally on your device to provide core functionality:

  • Game Progress: Your current challenge status and completed challenges
  • Statistics: Your scores, performance metrics, and game history
  • Achievements: Badges and milestones you've unlocked
  • Settings: Your app preferences and customizations
  • Game History: Record of your past games and results
Important: All data is stored locally using your device's secure storage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any servers.

4. How We Use Your Data

Since we don't collect any data from your device, we don't use, analyze, share, or sell any of your information. The data stored locally is used solely to:

  • Save your game progress between sessions
  • Display your statistics and achievements
  • Remember your app preferences
  • Provide a personalized experience within the app

All processing happens locally on your device.

5. Data Security

We take the security of your locally stored data seriously:

  • Local Storage: All data is stored in your device's secure app-specific storage
  • Encryption: Your device's operating system provides encryption for app data
  • No Network Transmission: Since no data is sent over the internet, there's no risk of interception
  • Offline Functionality: The app works completely offline, ensuring your data never leaves your device

6. Your Data Rights and Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: All your data is accessible within the app's interface
  • Modification: You can change or update your data by playing games and adjusting settings
  • Deletion: Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all locally stored data
  • Portability: Since data is stored locally, it remains on your device until you delete the app

7. Children's Privacy

Quick 5 is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13:

  • We do not knowingly collect any information from children or any users
  • The app does not require account creation or registration
  • There are no social features, chat functions, or ways to share personal information
  • The app complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)

Parents can feel confident that their children's privacy is protected when using Quick 5.

8. Third-Party Services

Quick 5 does not integrate with any third-party services, including:

  • No analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.)
  • No advertising networks
  • No social media platforms
  • No cloud storage services
  • No payment processors (the app is completely free)

9. International Users

Quick 5 can be used anywhere in the world. Since we don't collect or transfer any data:

  • There are no international data transfers
  • GDPR (European Union) compliance: No personal data is processed
  • CCPA (California) compliance: No personal information is collected or sold
  • All data remains on your device, regardless of your location
