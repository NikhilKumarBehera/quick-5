# Quick 5 - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** March 1, 2026

## Introduction

Welcome to Quick 5 ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application Quick 5 (the "App").

## Information We Collect

### Data We DO NOT Collect

Quick 5 is designed with privacy in mind. We **do not** collect, store, transmit, or share any of the following:

- Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Location data
- Device identifiers
- IP addresses
- Usage analytics
- Advertising data
- Cookies or similar tracking technologies
- Any data from third-party services

### Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All your app data is stored locally on your device and never leaves your device:

- **Game Progress:** Your completed challenges and current level
- **Statistics:** Your performance metrics and scores
- **Achievements:** Badges and milestones you've unlocked
- **Settings:** Your app preferences (sound, notifications, etc.)
- **History:** Your past game results

This data is stored using secure, encrypted local storage mechanisms provided by your device's operating system.

## How We Use Your Data

Since we don't collect any data, we don't use, share, or sell any data. All processing happens locally on your device.

## Data Security

Your local data is protected by:
- Your device's built-in security features
- Encrypted storage (when available on your device)
- No transmission over networks
- No third-party access

## Your Data Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control:

- **Access:** All data is viewable within the app
- **Modification:** You can change settings at any time
- **Deletion:** You can reset all data via the app settings or by uninstalling the app
